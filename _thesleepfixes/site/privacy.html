Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

The Sleep Fixes (thesleepfixes.com) is run by Kzam Studio, Saint-Nazaire, France. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you request the free guide, we collect your email address and, if you give it, your first name. We don't collect payment details. We don't sell your data.

What we use it for

We use your email to send you the guide and a short series of emails about sleep. Some of those emails mention products and include affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Who handles it

Our email list is stored and sent by our email service provider, Kit (kit.com). Kit processes your data on our behalf under its own privacy policy.

Unsubscribing and your rights

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click removes you. You can also ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to contact@thesleepfixes.com.

Health information

This site shares general information. It is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor about ongoing sleep problems or before starting any supplement.
